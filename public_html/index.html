Our Mission

It has come to our attention that children are more of an investment and especially to a country's growth. We have to focus on building them and guiding them in order to help them become the best they can be as individuals and citizens of our beloved country.

As the A Helping Hand NGO our aim is to help children strive to get quality education, access to the right nutrition and provide health care.

We have organized a group of willing women to go out of their way to look after children who are coming from poor communities. We go to their schools to give food, clothing, moral support and encouragement.

About Us

A Helping Hand is a small NGO organization made up of women and men who are committed and passionate about assisting those who are in need.

It was founded in 2001 by Leonardo Junior Langa whose aim was to minimize child abuse and inequality in underprivileged communities. We mainly assess and explore poor communities and determine how we can become of great use and support. Education, health, and child protection are the key areas we investigate.

We interact with children and provide protection because there are high levels of violence against women and children. Many cases go unreported and people suffer in silence.

Some communities experience high dropout rates because children struggle to get to school. School fees, hunger, and responsibilities at home often affect their academic performance. Many begin to believe school is useless, and that is where we step in to encourage and support learners.

Our Vision: To create a brighter tomorrow for deprived children.

Our Mission: To bring sustainable change to children's lives through education and opportunities that create a better future.

Our Work

We encourage children to become responsible. We nurture children's interests based on their choices and make learning fun. We give alternatives to the schools we visit by approaching teaching differently in customizable ways so that children can enjoy being in school.

We teach children their rights and how they should be properly brought up. We also arrange parent meetings so that parents can assist their children with learning, inspire them to think independently and help them become more receptive.

We provide clothing with the help of other organizations such as Jupiter Clothes and the government. We also give away food to schools such as Sedibeng Primary School, and travel around townships and villages to contribute to child care and protection.

What We Do

We play a part in helping children access quality education, care and progress in their early childhood. Nutrition and psycho-social support are promoted to reduce poverty and social inequality.
